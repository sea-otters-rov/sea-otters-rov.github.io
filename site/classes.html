The class ladder

Each class turns up the voltage, the current, and the difficulty. SCOUT and NAVIGATOR are regional-only classes — simpler and cheaper. RANGER wins at a regional to advance to the World Championship.

Above RANGER sit two advancing classes we don't compete in: PIONEER (advanced, 48 V) and EXPLORER (university level, 48 V / 30 A).

Our three classes at a glance

🔰

SCOUT

400 points. The simplest class: 12 V / 15 A, cameras optional, manual pumps only, no lasers. A hand-drawn wiring diagram is fine. Perfect for a first-year crew.

⚓

NAVIGATOR

450 points. A real step up: cameras required, powered fluid power allowed, lasers permitted, and a CAD wiring diagram is mandatory. The independent battery-powered float appears here.

🚀

RANGER

745 points. Nearly double the current (25 A), a 25 kg weight cap with a weight bonus, harder autonomous tasks, a mandatory JSEA safety document, and full Worlds-level documentation.

In every class, the paperwork is worth 40–50% of your total score — more than the pool run. Details in Scoring & Deliverables. And RANGER's hard design specs live where you'll need them: electrical and inspection rules on Safety, the weight cap and bonus in the Build Guide.

Our path up the ladder is deliberate: NAVIGATOR + SCOUT in 2027, RANGER in 2028 — with the budget, deep-water practice, and documentation habits to make it count. The full reasoning is in the Season Plan 2027.

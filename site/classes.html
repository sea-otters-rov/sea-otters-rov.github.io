The five-class ladder

Each class turns up the voltage, the current, and the difficulty. SCOUT and NAVIGATOR are regional-only classes — simpler and cheaper. RANGER, PIONEER, and EXPLORER advance to the World Championship.

Regional vs. World Championship

SCOUT & NAVIGATOR compete only at regionals. RANGER wins at a regional to advance to the World Championship, where (new for 2026) tasks are split across three real research venues in St. John's, Newfoundland — and you get only one attempt per task.

Our three classes at a glance

🔰

SCOUT

400 points. The simplest class: 12 V / 15 A, cameras optional, manual pumps only, no lasers. A hand-drawn wiring diagram is fine. Perfect for a first-year crew.

⚓

NAVIGATOR

450 points. A real step up: cameras required, powered fluid power allowed, lasers permitted, and a CAD wiring diagram is mandatory. The independent battery-powered float appears here.

🚀

RANGER

745 points. Nearly double the current (25 A), a 25 kg weight cap with a weight bonus, harder autonomous tasks, a mandatory JSEA safety document, and full Worlds-level documentation.

The surprising truth

In every class, the paperwork — technical documentation, presentation, marketing, safety — is worth 40–50% of your total score. That's bigger than the pool run. Rookie teams that treat documentation as an afterthought leave the single largest pile of points on the table. See Scoring & Deliverables.

Build light — the points are real

The swing from a <15 kg ROV to a 20+ kg one is a full 10-point loss — roughly a whole sub-task's worth of points. Over-built, heavy vehicles literally get penalized.

How to choose your class

  • First season ever, small budget, younger crew? Start with SCOUT. Optional cameras and manual pumps keep the build honest and cheap.
  • Comfortable with basic electronics, want cameras and a real float challenge? Step up to NAVIGATOR.
  • Ready to chase a World Championship berth? Commit to RANGER — but respect the extra load: the JSEA, CAD diagrams, tighter safety, and full documentation.

Our two teams finished 5th and 6th at the 2026 regional — a top-3 pool run held back by the paperwork. The natural path to the podium is to consolidate on RANGER while using SCOUT/NAVIGATOR knowledge as the foundation — the mission storyline and most safety rules are shared across all three.

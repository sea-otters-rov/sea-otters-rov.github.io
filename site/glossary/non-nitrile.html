Glossary

Non-nitrile

An O-ring made from any rubber other than nitrile, such as EPDM, silicone, or FKM (Viton).

Nitrile (also sold as NBR or Buna-N) is the default black O-ring: cheap, tough, and excellent with oil and fuel. Its weak spots are chlorine, ozone, and sunlight, which slowly harden and crack it. Then we need non-nitrile O-rings.

On an ROV

We dive in chlorinated pool water. A nitrile O-ring will seal fine for a session, but months of chlorine and drying in the sun age it, and a hardened ring leaks. EPDM handles chlorinated water and sunlight much better, which is why pool equipment uses it. Whatever the material, check each ring for cracks and flat spots, and replace worn ones.

Common confusion

The grease has to match the rubber. Petroleum grease (like Vaseline) swells EPDM, so use silicone grease on it. Most O-rings are black, so you cannot tell the material by looking; check the part listing.

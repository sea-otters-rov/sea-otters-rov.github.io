Glossary

AGM

Short for absorbent glass mat: a sealed lead-acid battery that cannot spill.

An AGM battery uses the same chemistry as a car battery, but its acid is soaked into fiberglass mats packed between the lead plates instead of sloshing around as a liquid. That makes it sealed, maintenance-free, and safe to mount in any position. The trade-off is weight.

On an ROV

The Navigator recommends and Ranger requires the float to carry its own battery, and the allowed types are NiMH and AGM. "Sealed" does not mean waterproof, so the battery still lives inside a watertight housing with a fuse. Take it out to charge it, because an overcharged AGM can vent hydrogen gas.

AGM vs. NiMH

AGM comes as one heavy 6 V or 12 V block and is cheap. NiMH is a pack of small 1.2 V cells: lighter and easier to fit in a narrow float.

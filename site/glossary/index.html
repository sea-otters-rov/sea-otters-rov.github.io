Glossary

Quick, plain-language definitions of the robotics terms that show up in our documents.

  • AGM (absorbent glass mat): a sealed, spill-proof battery
  • H-bridge: four switches that run a motor both ways
  • ID and OD (inner/outer diameter): how tubes are measured
  • LoRa (long range): low-power radio for small data packets
  • MCU (microcontroller): reads sensors and drives motors
  • Non-nitrile: O-rings made from rubber other than nitrile

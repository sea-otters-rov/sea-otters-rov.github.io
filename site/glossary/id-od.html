Glossary

ID and OD

Short for inner diameter and outer diameter: the two widths of anything round and hollow.

Look at the cut end of a tube. The ID is the width of the hole. The OD is the full width across, including both walls, so OD = ID + 2 × wall thickness. The same two numbers describe pipes, hoses, O-rings, washers, and bearings.

On an ROV

Fit depends on picking the right one. An end cap that plugs into an enclosure tube must match the tube's ID. A clamp or gripper that goes around a pipe must match its OD. O-rings are sold by ID and thickness.

Common confusion

Pipe names are not measurements. "½-inch PVC" is a trade size: the pipe is really about 0.84 in (21 mm) OD and 0.62 in ID. Measure with calipers before you design a part around it.
